lenskit.logging.ResourceMeasurement
===================================

.. py:class:: lenskit.logging.ResourceMeasurement
   :canonical: lenskit.logging._resource.ResourceMeasurement

   Single measurement of resources.  Two measurements can be subtracted to
   compute the time resources consumed in an interval (memory resources are
   left unchanged).
